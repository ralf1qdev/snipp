FAQ

Questions, answered.

Getting started

Is Snipp free?

Yes. Snipp is free to download and use, with no account, ads or trial period.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" when I run the installer.

That's Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, which warns about new downloads that haven't built up a reputation yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need administrator rights?

No. Snipp installs just for your user account, so there's no UAC prompt.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or Windows 11. Snipp uses the .NET Framework that's already part of Windows, so there's nothing else to install.

Using Snipp

PrtSc opens the Windows Snipping Tool instead of Snipp.

On Windows 11, PrtSc is assigned to the built-in tool by default. Turn off Settings › Accessibility › Keyboard › Use the Print screen key to open screen capture, or choose a different shortcut in Snipp's settings. Snipp tells you when it detects this.

Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Right-click the Snipp icon in the notification area, choose Settings, click a shortcut box and press the new combination.

How do I capture a menu or a tooltip?

Use Snip with delay from the tray menu (3, 5 or 10 seconds), open the menu, and wait — or simply press the shortcut while the menu is open; the screen is frozen instantly.

Text recognition says no language is installed.

Windows' OCR engine uses your display languages. Add a language with OCR support in Settings › Time & language › Language (English and most major languages qualify).

Can I edit an annotation after I've drawn it?

Yes. Choose the Select tool (V), click the annotation, then move it, drag its handles, change its color or size, or press Del. Double-click text to edit it.

Privacy & files

Does Snipp upload my screenshots anywhere?

No. Snipp never connects to the internet. Snips stay on your PC — on the clipboard, in the folder you choose, and in your local history (which you can turn off or clear). Text recognition also runs entirely offline.

Where are my snips stored?

Saved snips go to Pictures\Snipp by default (changeable in Settings). Recent-snip history lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Snipp\History and keeps the last 50 by default.

Does the online demo upload my edits?

No. The Try it editor runs entirely in your browser. Sample images are generated on the page and nothing you draw is sent anywhere.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Settings › Apps, find Snipp and choose Uninstall. Your saved screenshots are kept; you can choose whether to remove your settings too.
