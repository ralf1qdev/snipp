Install

  1. Open SnippSetup.exe from your downloads.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. New apps show this until they build up reputation.
  3. Pick the install folder and options (desktop shortcut, start with Windows), then click Install.
  4. Press PrtSc to take your first snip.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • .NET Framework 4.8 — already part of Windows 10 (1903+) and 11
  • About 1 MB of disk space
  • Text recognition needs a Windows display language with OCR support (English and most major languages)

Update

Download the latest installer and run it. It closes Snipp, updates it in place and keeps your settings and history.

Uninstall

Open Settings › Apps, find Snipp and choose Uninstall. Your saved screenshots are kept; you can choose whether to remove your settings too.

What's new
