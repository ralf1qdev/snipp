Why Snipp exists

Snipp started from a simple frustration: taking a screenshot, pointing at something and blurring a password shouldn't require a heavyweight app, an account or a cloud upload.

So Snipp is native, starts instantly and weighs about half a megabyte. It does the everyday things — capture, annotate, redact, copy text — really well, and stays out of your way the rest of the time.

What we care about

Speed

From key press to frozen screen with no delay. The editor opens instantly, even for multi-monitor snips.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your PC. No telemetry, no uploads, no account. Even text recognition is offline.

Respect

No ads, no nags, no bundled extras. Installs per-user and uninstalls cleanly.

Built with

Snipp is a native Windows app written in C# on the .NET Framework that ships with Windows, so there's no runtime to install. Text recognition uses the OCR engine built into Windows 10 and 11. The installer is custom-built and per-user — no admin prompt.
