Features

Small app. Big toolbox.

Capture anything on screen, explain it with annotations, hide what's private, and share it — all without leaving the keyboard.

Instant capture

One shortcut freezes the screen. Drag a region, click a window, or press Enter for a whole monitor.

Annotation tools

Pen, highlighter, lines, arrows, boxes, ellipses, text and numbered steps. Every mark stays editable.

Blur & pixelate

Hide emails, card numbers and faces before you share. Redaction changes the pixels, it doesn't just cover them.

Text recognition New

Copy text straight off the screen with Shift+PrtSc, or extract it from any snip. Runs offline.

Snip history New

Closed a snip without saving? Your recent snips are one click away in the history window.

Repeat last area New

Capture exactly the same region again with one shortcut — perfect for before/after shots.

Spotlight & callouts New

Dim everything except what matters, and point at details with speech-bubble callouts.

Pin to screen

Float any snip above your windows as a reference while you work. Resize it, fade it, drag it anywhere.

Frame & shadow New

One click puts your snip on a gradient backdrop with rounded corners — ready for slides and posts.

Magnifier & color picker

Pixel-perfect selection with a live magnifier, plus an eyedropper to reuse any color from the image.

Always ready

Lives quietly in the notification area and starts with Windows, so a snip is always one key away.

Tiny & private

About half a megabyte. No account, no ads, no telemetry, no internet connection required.

Capture

Select exactly what you need.

The screen freezes the moment you press the shortcut, so menus and tooltips stay put.

  • Drag a region with a live size readout
  • Hover and click to grab a window precisely
  • Magnifier with pixel color and coordinates
  • Arrow keys nudge the cursor one pixel at a time
  • Delayed snips (3, 5 or 10 s) for menus and hover states
  • Works across multiple monitors and high-DPI displays
Editor

Explain it, don't just show it.

Sixteen tools, every one a keystroke away. Annotations stay separate from the image, so you can move, resize, recolor or delete them any time.

  • Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter and numbered steps
  • Text labels and speech-bubble callouts
  • Blur, pixelate and spotlight
  • Crop, rotate, zoom and unlimited undo
Try the editor in your browser →
Text recognition

Copy text you can't select.

Grab text from images, videos, error dialogs and scanned documents. Recognition happens on your PC using the engine built into Windows.

  • Shift+PrtSc, select an area — the text is on your clipboard
  • Review and edit before copying
  • Extract text from any snip in the editor with Ctrl+E
Share

Looks great anywhere you paste it.

Copy straight to the clipboard, save as PNG, JPG, BMP or GIF, pin it on screen, or add a frame and shadow in one click.
